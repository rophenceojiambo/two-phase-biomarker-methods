MIDUS REAL-WORLD APPLICATION — PUBLIC ARCHIVE

This directory retains only the manuscript-relevant and QC outputs needed to
support the published real-world application. Script 45/47 may generate
additional convenience formats during development, but those are not required
in the frozen public archive.

Retained figure:
  figures/Figure_real_world_method_comparison_with_annotations.pdf
    Annotated coefficient/95% CI figure used as Figure 6 in the manuscript.

Retained tables/data:
  tables/Table3_characteristics_by_phase2.csv
    Characteristics by Phase-2 RNA-marker availability.

  tables/Table3_standardization_qc.csv
    QC for standardized variables used in Table 3.

  tables/Table4_phase2_correlations_display.csv
    Display-ready lower-triangle correlation table.

  tables/Table4_phase2_correlations_r.csv
  tables/Table4_phase2_correlations_p.csv
    Machine-readable correlation coefficients and p-values supporting Table 4.

  tables/Table_real_world_application.csv
  tables/Table_real_world_application_unrounded.csv
    Rounded and unrounded six-method real-world comparison summaries.

  tables/real_world_gtsummary_table_qc.csv
    QC for generated manuscript tables.

  diagnostics/real_world_weight_diagnostics_summary.csv
    IPW/AIPW weight diagnostics including effective-sample-size fraction.

Convenience Word files, alternate table layouts, duplicate PNG renderings,
the unannotated method-comparison figure, and exploratory method-difference
tables are intentionally excluded from the final public archive.

The clean reader-facing copies of the exact manuscript Figure 6 and Tables 3–4
are available under results/manuscript/main/.
